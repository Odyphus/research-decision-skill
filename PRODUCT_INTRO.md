# Research Decision Skill

Research Decision helps with the research judgment that matters now—and explains the expert cues behind it.

It uses the nonlinear D1–D29 map to locate the current decision, loads the matching STEM domain pack, verifies current public expert judgment, compares that judgment with the user's own reasoning, and recommends a bounded next action with explicit change conditions.

The default answer gives one conditional recommendation, the decisive basis, and one next action with observable continue, pivot, stop, or revisit conditions. It then offers optional help: explain the judgment, expand the expert evidence, compare live alternatives, or show the decision record.

`v0.5.0-preview` supports nine STEM domain states and remains a public Preview. Human domain-expert review and real learning-transfer effectiveness are `not_run`. Invoke it explicitly with `$research-decision-skill`.
