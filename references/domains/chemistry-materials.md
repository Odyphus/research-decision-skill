# Chemistry and materials science domain pack

Support status: `public_source_decision_support_preview`.

## Real tasks

Select synthesis conditions, controls, characterization, reference materials, property definitions, computational parameters, mechanism tests, stability checks, scale-up conditions, and the evidentiary threshold for a structure-property or reaction claim.

## Qualified expertise and source order

Prefer standards, validated methods, reference-material and instrument guidance, domain reporting checklists, primary experimental or computational papers with complete methods, and identifiable laboratories' protocols. Match expertise to the material class, phase, specimen preparation, instrument, and property.

## Evidence rules

- Unambiguously identify substance, composition, phase, specimen history, environment, units, state variables, and raw versus derived quantities.
- Record calibration, detection limits, blanks, reference materials, batches, replicates, uncertainty, and data-processing steps.
- A spectrum or image alone does not establish purity, identity, mechanism, bulk property, or generality.
- Computational claims require software/version, parameters, convergence, initial structures, and comparison to an appropriate reference or experiment.
- Separate repeatability within one batch from reproducibility across batches, instruments, or labs.

## Common decisions and failure modes

Typical decisions concern which characterization discriminates mechanisms, whether a property is intrinsic or process-dependent, and whether evidence justifies scale-up. Failures include ambiguous material identity, hidden batch effects, selective spectra, uncalibrated instruments, insufficient blanks, metastability, contamination, and incomparable processing histories.

## Search templates

- `<material/property> reference method uncertainty reproducibility`
- `<synthesis> batch effect failure analysis stability`
- `<characterization> validation detection limit interference`
- `<mechanism> discriminating experiment isotope control`

## Abstain boundary

Use `暂缓定论` when material identity, phase, processing history, calibration, units, raw data, or method parameters are missing, or when the proposed mechanism has no discriminating observation.
