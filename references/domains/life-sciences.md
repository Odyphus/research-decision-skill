# Life sciences domain pack

Support status: `public_source_decision_support_preview` for research decisions only.

## Real tasks

Define biological question and experimental unit, select model organism or system, controls, randomization, blinding, sample-size rationale, assay validation, batch strategy, exclusion rules, replication, causal tests, and claim scope.

## Qualified expertise and source order

Prefer ethics and regulatory requirements, established reporting and design guidance, validated protocols and assay standards, primary papers with complete methods, and identifiable domain experts' public protocols. Match expertise to organism, tissue, assay, disease model, intervention, and translational stage.

## Evidence rules

- Define biological and technical replicates, experimental unit, inclusion/exclusion, randomization, blinding, sex and other relevant biological variables, batch, endpoint, and analysis plan.
- Validate reagent, cell line, organism, assay specificity, dynamic range, and measurement reliability.
- Separate exploratory, confirmatory, mechanistic, and translational claims.
- An animal or in-vitro result does not establish human clinical benefit.
- This Skill does not diagnose patients, recommend treatment, or authorize clinical deployment.

## Common decisions and failure modes

Typical decisions concern the right biological model, decisive control, experimental unit, replication level, or mechanistic discriminator. Failures include pseudoreplication, batch confounding, unvalidated reagents, immortalized-line overgeneralization, selective exclusions, insufficient controls, endpoint switching, and overstated translation.

## Abstain boundary

Use `暂缓定论` when ethics approval or consent is required but unresolved, the experimental unit or model identity is unclear, an assay is unvalidated, or the request crosses into patient diagnosis, treatment, or clinical deployment. Route clinical questions to appropriate qualified professionals.
